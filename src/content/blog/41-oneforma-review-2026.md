## What Is OneForma?

OneForma is a crowdsourcing platform operated by Centific, a company formerly known as Pactera EDGE. Centific specializes in language services and AI training data — supplying labeled datasets, search evaluation results, and linguistic resources to technology companies building AI systems. OneForma is the contributor-facing side of this business: the platform through which thousands of independent contributors around the world complete the human tasks that generate this data.

The types of work available through OneForma span a broader range than most single-focus platforms:

**Search and AI Evaluation:** Rating the quality and relevance of search results, evaluating AI-generated content, and assessing whether digital products are meeting user intent. This is the same category of work that Appen and Telus International AI specialize in.

**Linguistic Tasks:** Transcription, translation review, language data collection, and voice recording. These tasks draw on language skills rather than purely evaluative judgment.

**Data Collection and Annotation:** Image labeling, audio recording, text annotation, and other structured data tasks that feed into AI training pipelines.

**Content Review:** Reviewing digital content against policy and quality guidelines.

The variety of task types means OneForma can offer work to a broader range of contributors than purely search-evaluation platforms — contributors with specific language skills or domain expertise find more relevant task options available.

## The Qualification Process

OneForma's qualification approach is task-specific rather than platform-wide. You do not pass a single test to access all tasks; instead, each task category has its own qualification assessment.

**Getting started:** Create an account on oneforma.com, complete your profile including language skills, country, and areas of expertise. Your profile information determines which task types you will be eligible to test for.

**Task qualifications:** When a task type becomes available for your profile, you receive an invitation to complete a qualification assessment. For search evaluation roles, this typically involves studying the guidelines document for that project and demonstrating you can apply them correctly. For linguistic tasks, the assessment tests your language proficiency or specific skill.

**Active work access:** Once you pass a qualification, you access that task type's work queue through the OneForma portal. Different task types have separate queues — qualifying for one does not automatically open others.

This modular system means contributors can build up access to multiple task types over time, each with its own qualification, rather than being approved or rejected for the entire platform at once. It also means that even if you qualify quickly for some task types, others in your queue may take time before an assessment becomes available.

## Pay Rates

OneForma's pay varies by task type, project, and region. For the main categories:

**Search and AI evaluation tasks** typically pay in the $12–$17 per hour range, comparable to similar work on Appen and Telus International AI. Rates reflect the guidelines complexity and required expertise for each specific project.

**Linguistic tasks** vary more. Translation review and language evaluation tasks for supported language pairs may pay different rates depending on the language rarity and task complexity. Less common languages tend to pay more per task than major languages due to supply constraints.

**Data annotation and collection tasks** vary significantly by task type. Straightforward annotation tasks pay less per hour than evaluation work; specialized tasks requiring domain expertise pay more.

Payment schedules and methods vary by region. OneForma processes payments monthly in most markets through bank transfer or payment processors. Specific payment details are confirmed during onboarding for each project.

## Task Availability

Like all project-based evaluation platforms, OneForma's available work fluctuates with active client contracts. Some periods see consistent task availability in your queue; others are quiet while projects are in review or between contract renewals.

OneForma's broader task variety — spanning evaluation, linguistic, and annotation work — theoretically provides more resilience against any single project type going quiet. In practice, not all task types are available in all regions, and the linguistic tasks in particular are constrained by language pair demand on the client side.

Contributors who have both general evaluation tasks and linguistic tasks qualified tend to experience more consistent overall availability than those limited to a single task category. If you have relevant language skills, qualifying for those tracks alongside evaluation work is worth pursuing.

## OneForma vs Appen vs Telus International AI

All three platforms operate in the same category — AI training data and search evaluation — and serve overlapping technology clients. Key practical differences:

**Scale:** Appen and Telus International AI have larger contributor bases and more projects running globally at any given time. OneForma is smaller but serves a genuine and consistent contributor base.

**Task variety:** OneForma's linguistic task options give it more breadth than Telus International AI (which focuses primarily on search rating). Appen also has a broad task mix; both Appen and OneForma offer more variety than Telus.

**Qualification approach:** Telus International AI's search quality rating requires studying the full Google Search Quality Rater Guidelines and a multi-part exam. OneForma's qualifications vary by task — some are shorter assessments, others are more involved. Appen's entry varies by project.

**Payment frequency:** Telus International AI pays more frequently than monthly in most markets. Appen pays monthly. OneForma typically pays monthly, though this varies by region.

**Simultaneous enrollment:** Working for all three simultaneously is the standard approach for experienced evaluation contributors. OneForma adds supply-side variety when hours are reduced on Appen or Telus International AI.

## Who OneForma Suits

OneForma is best suited to:

**Experienced evaluation contributors** who are already working on Appen or Telus International AI and want to add another platform to smooth out slow periods. The skills required are directly transferable, and the additional qualification process is straightforward for people already familiar with guidelines-based evaluation work.

**Contributors with multilingual skills** — OneForma's linguistic task options mean that speakers of less common languages have more relevant work available than on purely evaluation-focused platforms.

**Contributors in regions with limited Appen/Telus availability** — In some markets, OneForma has active projects when the major platforms are quiet. Regional variation affects all platforms, and having OneForma qualified provides a fallback.

OneForma is less suited to complete beginners who have no experience with guidelines-based evaluation work.
