Offerwalls generate more income per hour than surveys for most users, but they're also the most misunderstood section of reward platforms. This guide explains exactly how they work and how to get the most from them.

## What an Offerwall Actually Is

An offerwall is an advertising distribution system embedded within a rewards platform. Here's the chain:

1. A brand (a mobile game company, a subscription service, a market research firm) wants users to take a specific action
2. The brand pays an offerwall network to distribute that offer
3. The network places the offer inside reward platforms that users already visit
4. A user completes the offer → the brand's tracking system verifies completion → credits flow back through the network to the user's platform account

The platform keeps a portion of what the brand pays; you receive the rest as credits.

## Types of Offers Explained

### App Install + Achievement Offers (Highest Paying)
**How they work**: Install a mobile game or app via the offer link. Reach a specific in-game milestone (level 25, day 7 login, first purchase, etc.).
**Pay range**: $2–$30 depending on the milestone difficulty
**Time required**: 30 minutes (easy milestones) to several hours (high-level requirements)
**Key rule**: Only counts if installed through the offerwall link. Installing through the App Store directly = no credit.

### Registration / Sign-Up Offers
**How they work**: Create an account on a website or service, sometimes with email verification.
**Pay range**: $0.50–$5
**Time required**: 5–15 minutes
**Key rule**: Use a valid email you own. Some offers require holding an account for 7 days before credit fires.

### Survey Offers
**How they work**: Complete a market research survey about your habits, opinions, or demographics.
**Pay range**: $0.50–$5
**Time required**: 5–20 minutes
**Key rule**: Disqualification mid-survey doesn't usually earn credit — only full completion does.

### Video / Task Offers
**How they work**: Watch a video, complete a quiz, test a product.
**Pay range**: $0.05–$0.50
**Time required**: 1–5 minutes
**Notes**: Low individual value but can be done in bulk quickly.

## Why Offers Sometimes Don't Credit

The most common causes of missing credits:

**Ad blocker enabled**: Ad blockers block the tracking pixel that confirms you clicked from the offerwall. Always disable your ad blocker before starting an offer.

**VPN active**: If the advertiser expects traffic from India and your VPN shows a US IP, the tracking rejects the completion.

**Wrong device**: Started the offer on your phone but the game tracked progress on a tablet. Complete offers on one device.

**Pre-existing account**: Many app offers only work for new users. If you already have an account with the app, the offer won't credit.

**Credit delay**: Some offers credit immediately; others credit after 24–72 hours when the advertiser verifies the action. If credit doesn't appear immediately, wait 3 days before contacting support.

## Strategy for Maximising Offerwall Earnings

**1. Check offers in the morning**
New offers are added daily, and high-paying offers are completed quickly by other users. Checking in the morning gives you access to fresh inventory.

**2. Sort by highest paying, not easiest**
Most offerwall interfaces let you sort by reward value. A $10 game milestone offer that takes 2 hours is better than ten $0.50 survey offers that take the same time.

**3. Use multiple offerwall providers**
Different offerwall networks (CPX Research, ClixWall, AdGem, TheoremReach, MyLead) carry different offers. Platforms that integrate multiple networks give you more options daily.

**4. Read the completion requirements before starting**
Some offers have strict conditions — "reach level 20 within 14 days" or "make a purchase within 7 days." Know what you're committing to.

**5. Take screenshots of completion**
Before and after. If an offer doesn't credit and you need to contact support, a screenshot of the completed state is your evidence.
