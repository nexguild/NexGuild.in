Data annotation is the invisible work behind every AI product you use. When a photo app recognises your face, when a voice assistant understands what you said, when a spam filter catches junk mail — humans trained those systems by annotating data. This is a growing market with a genuine shortage of workers, which is why entry-level positions are accessible.

## What Data Annotators Do

Annotation is the process of adding labels to raw data so that AI models can learn from it. Depending on what you're annotating:

**Image annotation**: Drawing bounding boxes around cars, pedestrians, or road signs in photos for self-driving car training. Or labelling whether an image contains a cat, a chair, or inappropriate content.

**Text annotation**: Labelling whether a sentence expresses positive, negative, or neutral sentiment. Identifying named entities (people, places, organisations) in text. Classifying intent in customer queries.

**Audio annotation**: Transcribing spoken words, marking where specific sounds occur, labelling speaker gender or emotion.

**Video annotation**: Frame-by-frame object tracking for surveillance or autonomous systems.

**AI output review**: Checking whether a chatbot's response is accurate, helpful, and safe — a rapidly growing category as companies validate their AI systems.

## Where to Find Data Annotation Jobs

### Freelance Platforms (Start Here)

These accept new applicants continuously and have the lowest barrier to entry:

**Appen** — the world's largest annotation workforce. Tasks pay $9–$15/hour. Application involves a simple test. Work is project-based; availability fluctuates.

**Telus International** — similar to Appen. Known for search evaluation projects. Pay $12–$18/hour. Strong pipeline of tasks for English speakers.

**Remotasks** — beginner-friendly onboarding with free training courses. Pay $3–$8/hour (lower than Appen/Telus but more consistent availability).

**DataAnnotation.tech** — focuses on AI training data specifically. $15–$25/hour for writing, evaluating, and correcting AI responses. Requires application and writing sample.

**Scale AI** — higher pay ($18–$30/hour) but more competitive application process. Requires domain expertise for some tasks.

### Full-Time or Contract Roles

Companies including Amazon, Apple, Microsoft, and Meta hire annotation teams directly. These roles typically pay $15–$25/hour with benefits but are less flexible than freelance platforms.

Search LinkedIn, Indeed, or remote job boards with terms like "data annotator remote" or "AI trainer remote."

## How to Get Your First Annotation Job With No Experience

**Step 1: Create accounts on Appen and Remotasks simultaneously**

Both have open applications most of the time. Appen's application is longer (basic English and comprehension test). Remotasks accepts almost anyone and lets you start with free training courses.

**Step 2: Complete Remotasks training courses**

Remotasks offers free online courses for each annotation type (image segmentation, point cloud annotation, etc.). Completing these increases your access to higher-paying task categories.

**Step 3: Build your accuracy rate**

In the first weeks, focus on accuracy over speed. High accuracy unlocks higher-paying task types and keeps your account active.

**Step 4: Apply to Appen, Telus, and DataAnnotation.tech once you have experience**

A few weeks of Remotasks history makes your application more credible. DataAnnotation.tech in particular values demonstrated attention to detail.
