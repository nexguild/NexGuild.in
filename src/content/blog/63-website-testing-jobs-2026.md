Website and app testing is one of the highest-paying no-experience remote work options available. You get paid to use websites and apps exactly as a real user would — and tell the company what confuses you.

## What Website Testing Actually Involves

Companies spend enormous amounts building products but struggle to see them through a fresh user's eyes. They hire testers to catch usability problems before they frustrate real customers.

A typical test session:
1. You're given a website URL and 3–5 tasks to complete (e.g., "find a return policy")
2. You complete the tasks while speaking your thoughts aloud into your microphone
3. Your screen and voice are recorded
4. You optionally answer follow-up questions
5. You get paid

That's it. No technical knowledge required — companies want feedback from real users, not experts.

## The Best Website Testing Platforms in 2026

### UserTesting
**Pay:** $10 per 20-minute test ($30/hour)  
**Payout:** PayPal, 7 days after test approval  
**Availability:** Moderate — depends on demographic demand  

UserTesting is the largest and most established platform. Tests come from major brands (Fortune 500 companies regularly use it). The downside: testers are in high supply, so test availability fluctuates. Building a strong rating history unlocks access to more and higher-paying tests.

### TryMyUI
**Pay:** $10 per 20-minute test  
**Payout:** PayPal, weekly  
**Availability:** Lower than UserTesting  

TryMyUI is a smaller alternative with similar pay rates. Good for supplementing UserTesting access rather than replacing it.

### Testbirds
**Pay:** €10–€50 per test  
**Payout:** PayPal  
**Availability:** Variable, project-based  

Testbirds focuses on more complex testing scenarios — specific device types, real-world environments, structured feedback forms. Tests can pay significantly more than the standard 20-minute format.

### Userlytics
**Pay:** $5–$90 per session  
**Payout:** PayPal  
**Availability:** Project-based  

Userlytics occasionally runs live interviews (moderated tests) that pay $30–$90. These require video calling capability and scheduled appointments but pay significantly more than recorded tests.

### Enroll (formerly Validately)
**Pay:** $30–$120 per session  
**Payout:** Check or gift card  
**Availability:** Lower  

Enroll runs longer, more structured research sessions. Lower availability but much higher pay per session.

## What Equipment Do You Need?

**For desktop testing:**
- Computer with Chrome or Firefox
- External or built-in microphone (headset preferred)
- A stable internet connection
- Quiet environment

**For mobile testing:**
- Compatible smartphone (testers specify Android or iOS)
- Stable internet connection

Most platforms provide a free screen recording tool — you don't need to install separate software.

## How to Get Your First Test

1. **Sign up** on UserTesting or TryMyUI
2. **Complete the practice test** — this is mandatory and teaches you the think-aloud format
3. **Pass the screening test** — a sample test evaluated for audio quality and articulation
4. **Set your demographic profile** — age, occupation, tech skills. Tests are matched to your profile.
5. **Check for tests daily** — early morning often has the most availability

Your first few tests may be at lower pay rates while you build your rating score. Consistently clear audio and on-task completion builds your reputation quickly.
