UPI has made small digital payments practical in India, and earning apps that pay directly to UPI remove the friction of PayPal or bank transfer waits. This guide ranks the best options for 2026 based on actual pay rates, withdrawal speed, and reliability.

## What to Look for in a UPI Earning App

Not all "earning apps" are equal. Before installing any app, check:
- **Withdrawal method**: does it actually support UPI, or just PayPal and gift cards?
- **Minimum payout**: how much do you need to earn before withdrawing?
- **Processing time**: how long between requesting and receiving?
- **Task availability**: are there enough tasks to earn consistently?

---

## 1. TaskBucks

**What it pays for:** Completing short tasks, app downloads, surveys  
**Payout method:** UPI, Paytm, Mobikwik  
**Minimum payout:** ₹30  
**Processing time:** 24–48 hours

TaskBucks is one of the most UPI-native earning platforms in India. Tasks include installing apps, watching videos, filling short surveys, and referring friends. Earnings per task are small (₹1–₹10), but the volume is high and the UPI withdrawal is fast.

**Best for:** Daily passive earning from phone during spare time.

---

## 2. EarnKaro

**What it pays for:** Affiliate commissions from Flipkart, Amazon, Myntra, and 150+ brands  
**Payout method:** UPI, bank transfer  
**Minimum payout:** ₹10  
**Processing time:** 24 hours

EarnKaro is an affiliate marketing platform — you share product links and earn a commission when someone buys through your link. This model works well for anyone with an active social media presence or WhatsApp group. The ₹10 minimum payout is one of the lowest available.

**Best for:** Anyone who already shares product links or recommendations with friends and family.

---

## 3. CashKaro

**What it pays for:** Cashback on online shopping across 1,500+ stores  
**Payout method:** UPI, bank transfer  
**Minimum payout:** ₹250  
**Processing time:** 3–5 business days

CashKaro gives you cashback every time you shop through their tracked links at Flipkart, Amazon, Nykaa, Swiggy, and many others. Unlike task apps, this earns you money on purchases you'd already make.

**Best for:** Regular online shoppers who want passive cashback.

---

## 4. Rozdhan

**What it pays for:** Reading news, watching videos, daily check-ins, referrals  
**Payout method:** Paytm, UPI  
**Minimum payout:** ₹50  
**Processing time:** 1–3 days

Rozdhan gamifies passive content consumption. Reading news articles and watching short videos earns coins redeemable for Paytm or UPI transfers. Not high-earning, but completely passive.

**Best for:** People who scroll news and social media anyway.

For most users, Amazon India vouchers are practically equivalent to UPI for online spending.
