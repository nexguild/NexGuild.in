Finishing a survey only to find that the reward is missing is frustrating. On NexGuild, CPX Research rewards are credited automatically after the provider verifies a qualifying completion. If you do not see the NexCoins yet, use the steps below to check what happened and report the issue with the details support needs.

> **Missing a CPX Research reward?**
>
> Check your NexCoins balance and earnings history first. If the credit is still missing, contact support with the survey details so the completion can be reviewed.
>
> [Open NexGuild support →](/dashboard/support)

## 1. Make sure the survey actually reached its completion screen

Return to the survey page and check whether it showed a completion or thank-you message. Being screened out, closing the page before the final confirmation, or seeing an error is different from a confirmed completion and may not qualify for the full reward.

If you saw a clear completion confirmation, note that before closing the page. A screenshot can help, but do not share screenshots that expose passwords, private survey answers, or other sensitive information.

## 2. Check both your balance and earnings history

Refresh your NexGuild dashboard and check your NexCoins balance and transaction or earnings history. Sometimes the balance view takes a moment to update. Confirm that you are signed into the same NexGuild account you used to start the survey.

Verified completions are normally processed automatically, often within minutes. Provider verification and system processing can vary, so check again after a short wait. If the credit still does not appear, continue to the next step rather than starting the same survey again.

## 3. Save the details before contacting support

Write down as much of the following as you can:

- The survey provider: CPX Research
- The approximate completion date and time, including your timezone
- The survey reference or transaction ID, if one was displayed
- The NexCoin reward shown before you started
- What happened at the end of the survey, including any completion confirmation or error
- A screenshot of the completion page, if available

Do not guess a reference number or send your password, one-time codes, or payment credentials.

## 4. Contact NexGuild support

Use the [NexGuild support page](/dashboard/support) and explain that a CPX Research completion is missing from your earnings. Include the details above and the email address associated with your account.

Support can review the information and check whether a qualifying completion was reported. Because survey completion is verified by the provider, a reward cannot be promised unless the completion can be confirmed. Avoid opening multiple tickets for the same survey; add any new information to the existing request.

## Why a survey may not appear as a completed reward

Several different outcomes can look similar when you leave a survey:

- **A confirmed completion:** The survey showed that you finished. The provider still needs to verify and report the qualifying completion.
- **A screen-out:** Your answers did not match the study's audience, or its quota was full. This is a normal part of survey participation and does not necessarily earn the advertised completion reward.
- **An interrupted session:** The browser closed, the connection dropped, or the survey did not return to the platform's completion page. In that case, the provider may not have registered a completed survey.
- **A quality review or reversal:** A provider may review a response and decline or reverse its reward if it does not meet the survey's quality requirements.

For general background, read [how CPX Research works](/earn/blog/what-is-cpx-research-complete-guide) and [why surveys disqualify respondents](/earn/blog/why-survey-disqualification-happens-what-to-do).

## How to reduce tracking problems next time

- Start surveys from the CPX Research panel inside your NexGuild account.
- Keep the survey open until you see its final confirmation and return to NexGuild.
- Avoid opening the same survey in multiple tabs or restarting it after a confirmed completion.
- Use a stable connection and keep your browser updated.
- Answer honestly and follow the survey instructions; never use fabricated answers or attempt to bypass screening.

These steps can help prevent avoidable interruptions, but they cannot guarantee that every survey will match your profile or result in a reward. Survey availability, eligibility, and verification are controlled by each study and may vary by region.

## Quick checklist

1. Did the survey show a clear completion confirmation?
2. Did you refresh your NexGuild balance and check earnings history?
3. Did you note the time, expected reward, and any survey reference?
4. If the reward is still missing, did you send one support request with those details?
