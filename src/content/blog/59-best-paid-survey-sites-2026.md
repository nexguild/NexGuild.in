Most survey sites pay, but the earnings gap between the best and worst platforms is significant. This ranked list focuses on platforms with consistent payouts, honest pay rates, and low minimum withdrawal thresholds.

## What Makes a Survey Site Worth Using

Before the list: the two things that actually determine your hourly rate on any survey platform are **disqualification rate** and **pay per completed survey**. A platform that pays $1 per survey but disqualifies you 80% of the time is worse than one that pays $0.50 per survey with a 30% disqualification rate.

---

## 1. Prolific (Best Overall Pay Rate)

**Pay rate:** $6–$9/hour average  
**Minimum payout:** $5 via PayPal  
**Disqualification:** Very low — surveys are pre-screened to your profile

Prolific is run by academic and professional researchers, not marketing companies. Surveys must meet ethical standards and pay fairly by design. The platform pre-screens surveys before showing them to you, so disqualification mid-survey is rare.

The downside: survey availability is lower than commercial platforms, and it requires connecting a valid student or professional email for some studies.

---

## 2. Survey Junkie (Best for Volume)

**Pay rate:** $0.50–$3 per survey  
**Minimum payout:** $10 via PayPal or gift card  
**Disqualification:** Medium

Survey Junkie has one of the largest inventories of available surveys. The platform is honest about its points system (100 points = $1), and the minimum payout is reachable within a few weeks of regular use. Available in the US, Canada, and Australia.

---

## 3. CPX Research (Best for Integration with Reward Platforms)

**Pay rate:** Varies by platform, typically $0.50–$5 per survey  
**Minimum payout:** Depends on the host platform  
**Disqualification:** Medium — demographic-based screening

CPX Research powers the survey sections inside hundreds of earning platforms, reward apps, and micro-task sites. Rather than using CPX Research directly, you access it through a host platform that also offers other earning methods. This means your survey earnings contribute to a broader balance you can redeem for vouchers or cash.

NexGuild integrates CPX Research alongside offerwalls and tasks.

---

## 4. Swagbucks (Best for Variety)

**Pay rate:** $0.25–$2 per survey (lower than pure survey sites)  
**Minimum payout:** $3 via PayPal or gift card  
**Disqualification:** High

Swagbucks is not primarily a survey site — it also rewards you for watching videos, shopping, and searching. This diversification means even a bad survey day doesn't result in zero earnings. The downside is lower survey rates than specialist platforms.

---

## 5. Toluna (Best Global Coverage)

**Pay rate:** Points-based, roughly $0.50–$2 per survey  
**Minimum payout:** Varies by country  
**Disqualification:** Medium

Toluna operates in 68 countries and is one of the few platforms with consistent availability in South Asia, Africa, and Southeast Asia. The community features (creating your own surveys, voting) add an engagement layer most platforms lack.

---

## 6. Attapoll (Best Mobile App)

**Pay rate:** £0.10–£3 per survey  
**Minimum payout:** £1 via PayPal  
**Disqualification:** High

Attapoll's major advantage is the £1 minimum payout — you reach it quickly even as a new user. The mobile-first design works well for completing surveys during commutes. Disqualification rates are high, but this is typical of all survey apps.

## How to Maximize Survey Earnings

**Use 2–3 platforms simultaneously.** When one platform has no available surveys, others do. Most serious survey takers use Prolific + Survey Junkie + one mobile app.

**Complete your profile on every platform.** Surveys are matched to demographic profiles. Missing profile data = more disqualifications.

**Prioritize longer surveys.** A 20-minute $2 survey ($6/hour) is better than a 5-minute $0.20 survey ($2.40/hour).
