Lionbridge AI sits at the top tier of micro-task compensation — the pay rates are genuinely good and the work is more intellectually engaging than most platform tasks. But the application process is serious, and availability outside North America and Europe is inconsistent.

## What Lionbridge AI Does

Lionbridge AI collects human-generated data and evaluations to train and improve AI systems for its clients — primarily large technology companies. The work falls into several categories:

**Search Engine Evaluation (most common)**
You're given a real search query and a list of results. Your job is to rate how relevant, accurate, and trustworthy each result is. This data trains search algorithms to return better results.

**AI Training Data Collection**
Creating datasets — writing example sentences, recording audio samples, describing images — that AI models use to learn.

**Content Review and Rating**
Evaluating whether AI-generated content meets quality, accuracy, and safety standards.

**Map and Navigation Tasks**
Verifying that map data, business listings, and local information are accurate.

## The Application Process

**Step 1: Find an open position**

Go to lionbridge.com/ai-data-solutions and look for open "Evaluation" or "AI Trainer" roles. Positions are listed by language and region. If nothing is open in your region, check back monthly — projects rotate.

**Step 2: Read the guidelines document**

Once you apply and are accepted to the exam stage, you'll receive a guidelines document (often 100+ pages for search evaluation tasks). This is not optional reading — the exam tests your ability to apply specific rating criteria from the guidelines.

**Time to study**: Most successful applicants spend 15–20 hours with the guidelines before taking the exam.

**Step 3: Pass the qualification exam**

The exam consists of multiple-choice and practice rating tasks judged against gold-standard answers. Passing typically requires 70–80% accuracy. You usually get 1–2 attempts.

**Step 4: Complete training tasks**

After passing, you complete a batch of training tasks that are evaluated for accuracy. Maintain the required quality threshold (usually 70–75%) to stay active.

## What the Work Actually Pays

Search Engine Evaluation (US market): **$14–$16/hour equivalent**  
AI Training Data: **$12–$18/hour**  
Map tasks: **$10–$14/hour**

Pay is calculated per task completed, not by the hour. The effective hourly rate depends on how fast you work and how familiar you are with the rating criteria.

## Hours and Availability

Lionbridge AI is not a full-time job for most contributors. Projects have **weekly hour caps** (often 20–25 hours per week) and **availability fluctuates** based on project demand. Some weeks have plenty of tasks; others are near-zero.

This makes Lionbridge best as a high-quality supplement to other income, not a standalone primary earner.

## Who Should Apply

**Good fit:**
- Native or near-native English speakers (for English-language projects)
- People who can dedicate time to studying the guidelines seriously
- Those in the US, Canada, UK, Ireland, or Western Europe (most active hiring regions)

**Not a good fit:**
- People expecting to start earning immediately — the application takes 2–4 weeks
- Non-English markets where project availability is limited
- Anyone looking for daily task availability without gaps
