UHRS is one of the highest-paying task types available through Clickworker, but most contributors never access it because they don't know the qualification process exists. This guide explains what UHRS is, how to pass the assessment, and what the work actually involves.

## What Is UHRS?

UHRS stands for **Universal Human Relevance System** — it's Microsoft's crowdsourcing platform used to improve Bing search results, Cortana, and other Microsoft AI products. Rather than managing thousands of individual workers directly, Microsoft uses vendor companies like Clickworker to supply qualified contributors.

The tasks involve human judgment that AI cannot reliably perform:
- **Search relevance rating**: given a search query and a result, rate how relevant the result is
- **Image labeling**: identify objects, text, or attributes in images
- **Content quality rating**: evaluate whether AI-generated content meets quality standards
- **Intent classification**: determine what a user was trying to accomplish with a query

These are called **HitApps** inside the UHRS system.

## Step-by-Step: How to Access UHRS Through Clickworker

**Step 1: Create and verify your Clickworker account**

Go to clickworker.com, register, and complete your profile. You'll need to complete the basic Clickworker assessment (a writing and comprehension test) to unlock higher-paying task categories.

**Step 2: Look for UHRS qualification tasks in your job list**

Once your Clickworker account is approved, UHRS qualification tests appear as special jobs in your dashboard. They're labeled as "assessments" or "qualification tests." Check your job list daily — availability varies.

**Step 3: Pass the HitApp qualification**

Each UHRS HitApp has its own qualification test, typically 10–30 questions long. These tests are not easy — they test whether you can apply the rating guidelines consistently. You'll be given a guidelines document to read before starting.

**Key tips for passing:**
- Read the guidelines document completely before starting. Most failures come from skimming it.
- Guidelines are precise — "Excellent," "Good," "Fair," "Bad" ratings have specific definitions
- When unsure between two ratings, re-read the definition for each
- You typically get 2–3 attempts if you fail

**Step 4: Complete the onboarding tasks**

After passing, you'll complete a small batch of onboarding tasks (also evaluated against a gold standard). Maintain accuracy above the required threshold to stay active.

## What the Work Looks Like Day-to-Day

Once qualified for a HitApp, you access tasks through the UHRS portal (linked from your Clickworker dashboard). Each task typically takes 30–90 seconds. You work at your own pace — no fixed hours.

**Availability varies significantly.** Some days a HitApp has hundreds of tasks; other days it has zero. UHRS work comes in waves tied to Microsoft's internal project schedules. This unpredictability is the main downside.

Clickworker pays UHRS earnings the same way as regular tasks — via PayPal or SEPA transfer. Minimum payout is $5. Payments are processed on the 1st of each month for earnings from the previous month.
