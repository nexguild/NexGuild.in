## Step 1: Qualify for UHRS (The Biggest Unlock)

UHRS — Universal Human Relevance System — is Microsoft's platform for search relevance and AI evaluation tasks. Clickworker is one of the primary access points for UHRS work, and UHRS tasks are the most consistently available, highest-paying task type on the platform.

To access UHRS on Clickworker:

1. **Log in to your Clickworker account** and navigate to the UHRS section from the main dashboard.
2. **Choose a topic** — UHRS tasks are grouped by topic (web search evaluation, image relevance, content classification). Each topic has a separate qualification test.
3. **Read the topic guidelines** before attempting the test. The tests are not general knowledge — every correct answer is defined by the guidelines. Skipping this step is the most common reason people fail.
4. **Complete the qualification test** — typically 20–50 items that test your ability to apply the guidelines consistently.
5. **Wait for results** — passing unlocks access to live UHRS tasks for that topic.

Most contributors qualify on their first or second attempt when they study the guidelines first. Retakes are allowed after a waiting period (typically 24–72 hours).

## Step 2: Complete All Available Skill Assessments

Beyond UHRS, Clickworker has a broader skills assessment system. Your assessed skill level determines which task types you can access. Contributors with more completed and passed assessments unlock a wider range of tasks.

From your Clickworker profile:
- Go to "My Skills" or "Assessments"
- Complete every available assessment for skills you genuinely have
- Don't rush assessments — the scores are permanent and determine your task access tier

Key assessments to prioritize: **text quality**, **data entry accuracy**, **search evaluation** (if available in your region), and any language assessments matching languages you're proficient in.

## Step 3: Check Availability at Peak Times

Clickworker task availability follows daily and weekly patterns:

- **Higher availability:** Monday to Thursday, 8am–6pm in major advertiser time zones (US EST, EU CET)
- **Lower availability:** Weekends, early morning/late evening
- **Campaign spikes:** Some task types surge when clients post new campaigns — checking in mid-morning often catches these

This is not a guarantee, but regular contributors who check at consistent times report finding tasks more reliably than those who check sporadically.

## Step 4: Keep Your Completion Rate High

Clickworker's task routing considers your historical quality and completion rate. Contributors with higher quality scores and completion rates are prioritized when task slots are limited.

Protect your quality score by:
- Only accepting tasks you can complete to spec
- Reading instructions before starting (not after)
- Not abandoning tasks once started
- Completing tasks in one session when possible

A single bad quality score is harder to recover from than a slow day with no tasks.

## Step 5: Enable All Notification Types

Clickworker can notify you when new task types matching your qualifications become available. Enable email and browser notifications from your account settings so you don't miss new campaigns before they fill.

Contributors who respond quickly to task availability notifications consistently find more work per week than those who log in and check manually.

## What NOT to Do

A few approaches that don't help and can hurt:

- **Creating multiple accounts** — prohibited by Clickworker's terms and results in permanent bans.
- **Rushing through assessments** — lower scores reduce your task access, sometimes permanently.
- **Accepting tasks above your skill level** — low completion quality tanks your routing score.
- **Waiting for tasks to come to you** — active daily checking and notification setup are both necessary.
